FAQ

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

What exactly is Voxel World Pixel?

A collection of 1,111 pixel-art characters across 12 species — robot, cat, astronaut, alien, wizard, ninja, king, panda, frog, pirate, vampire and dino. Every piece is generated on a 16×16 pixel canvas with its own trait combination, so no two Voxels are alike.

How is the 1,111 supply split?

1,000 pieces are Free Mint for Binance Agent ID holders (max 4 per wallet), and 111 are Paid Mint Genesis editions at 0.0015 BNB (max 5 per wallet). Once they're gone, the supply is closed forever.

Who can use the Free Mint?

Only holders of a Binance Agent ID. Free Mint is gated: your wallet must hold a Binance Agent ID, and the check runs before the mint — on this preview page it is simulated in the wallet panel, and once the contract is live it verifies on-chain. No Binance Agent ID, no Free Mint — but Genesis stays open to everyone, no ID needed, up to 5 per wallet. The Free Mint draw itself is random, from the Common and Rare pool only — Free Mint never yields a Legendary.

Why mint a Genesis instead of a free one?

Genesis is the only mint with a direct shot at Legendary from its first draw. Every Genesis mint draws from the full pool — including Legendary pieces like the Pixel King — right at mint open, with boosted odds for Rare and Legendary traits such as crowns, laser eyes and gold sunburst worlds. Every Genesis also carries a permanent Genesis 111 badge plus priority access to future drops, including the Voxel World 2 airdrop. Free Mint is great for joining the world, but it draws randomly from Common and Rare only — no Legendary.

Can a Paid Mint get a Legendary right away?

Yes — it can, from its very first draw. Legendary is already in the Genesis draw when mint opens, not locked and not saved for a separate phase, so a Genesis mint can land a Legendary immediately. It is a direct chance on every Genesis draw, which is why Genesis is limited to just 111 pieces.

How does upgrading work?

Burn 2 of your own Voxels and pay 0.001 BNB to receive 1 brand-new Evolved character — an exclusive form that can never be minted directly. The Evolved result is guaranteed at least +1 rarity tier above the best piece you burned — unless that piece is already Legendary, in which case the result stays Legendary, because Legendary is the top tier. Total ever minted stays 1,111 — upgrading burns the 2 originals, so supply only shrinks. Evolved Voxels also get a real bonus in Pixel Playground: +1 life (you start with 4 lives).

Can an Evolved Voxel be Legendary?

Not from Free Mint pieces — an Evolved made from Free Voxels caps at Epic, no matter what you burn. Legendary stays exclusive to Genesis: the only way to hold a Legendary is the Genesis Paid Mint draw (or evolving Genesis material). That keeps the Genesis 111 special.

When does the Paid Mint open?

At launch — the Genesis Paid Mint (111 pieces at 0.0015 BNB) opens at exactly the same time as the Free Mint, not in a later phase. The contract is still TBA, so neither mint is live yet and the minting on this page is a preview simulation; once the contract is live, you will be able to mint a Genesis — and draw for Legendary — from day one.

How does minting via AI Agent work?

Copy the prompt, paste it to your agent, done. The prompt already contains everything for one fast execution — BNB, the contract, a single mint() call, and the exact fee (0 BNB for Free, 0.0015 BNB / 1500000000000000 wei for Genesis) — and the agent returns the transaction link. Contract is currently TBA: replace the placeholder in the prompt with the real address once the contract is live. You can rehearse the flow in the simulator on this page.

Do I need any ID to mint Genesis?

No. Paid Genesis is open to all wallets — no ID, no whitelist check. Connect any BNB wallet and mint directly, up to 5 per wallet.

Who can play Pixel Playground, and how do stages work?

Only Voxel holders can play. Connect your wallet — if it has minted a Voxel on this page (by wallet or AI Agent), the game unlocks and shows “Holder verified.” Until the contract is live, the holder check runs on this page's mint record, not on-chain; a clearly labelled demo preview is also available without an NFT. There are 5 stages — Night Meadow, Sunset Dunes, Neon Forest, Lava Caves and King's Sky. Each stage has a gem target and a time limit; reach the target before time runs out, with lives left, to clear it and unlock the next stage.

Which wallets are supported?

Any major Web3 wallet — MetaMask, Rabby, Trust Wallet and Coinbase Wallet included. Just set your wallet to BNB and use the Connect Wallet button in the top-right corner to get started.
